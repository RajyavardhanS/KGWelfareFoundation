# Replacing the illustrated placeholders

Four slots use SVG illustrations because no matching KGWF photographs were
supplied. Real KGWF photographs are always better. If you choose to use
AI-generated images instead, mark them as illustrative so they are not taken
for documentation of real beneficiaries or events.

To replace one, save the image as a JPEG (at least 1600 px on the long side,
4:5 portrait) and update the path in `src/content/programmes.ts` /
`src/content/stories.ts` (and the `Illustration` label will disappear for any
non-`.svg` image).

| `public/images/illustrations/clean-up-drive.svg` | Sustainability gallery | Documentary photograph, community volunteers with gloves and reusable sacks cleaning a lane in an old Jaipur neighbourhood with sandstone arches, residents joining in, soft afternoon light, candid, respectful, no text |

Style for all: natural colour grade, no heavy filters, people shown doing
rather than receiving, faces at eye level.
