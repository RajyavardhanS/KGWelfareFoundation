Separation rule for numbers
Community programme figures and Learning & Leadership (corporate) figures never share one grid without a label naming their vertical. Corporate percentages (45%, 40%, 80%, 85%, 30%, 35%) appear only on the Corporate / Learning page, each with its stated source.
Two frameworks, kept apart
Identify → Engage → Equip → Enable → Follow through is a design device for the community site, labelled as illustrative. The KGWF 6D Framework™ (Discover, Design, Deliver, Practice, Coach, Measure) is the official corporate-learning method and appears only in that vertical.
