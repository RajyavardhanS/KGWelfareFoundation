Why we do this

“We put sewing machines in the hands of women so they never have to depend on anyone. We teach photography to kids who’ve never held a camera. We clean neighbourhoods alongside the people who live there.”

We are not professional fundraisers. We are practitioners — a military officer, corporate professionals, educators, and community leaders. We plant trees, distribute food, counsel college students, and train youth for jobs — because we can. And because someone should.
