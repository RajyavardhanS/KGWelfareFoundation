Motion
220 ms ease-out. Image hover scale 1.03, title shifts 4 px, underline draws in. Numbers count once on first view. No parallax, no scroll-jacking, no floating blobs. All motion off under prefers-reduced-motion.
Photography
Real KGWF moments only. Faces at eye level, people doing, not receiving. Light warm grade, no heavy filters. One strong photograph beats a collage. Children’s faces only with documented consent.
Voice
Practitioner, not fundraiser. Short declaratives, concrete verbs (“we put sewing machines in the hands of women”). No pity, no superlatives, no corporate jargon. The source text is quoted, not rewritten.
